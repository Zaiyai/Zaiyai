## Why I Code 💡

  There is something special about taking an idea that exists only in your head and turning it into something that people can actually use, play with, or enjoy. And just as fulfilling as creating something is seeing someone else find joy in using it.

  I want to keep creating things that bring that feeling to others, while encouraging more people to discover it for themselves. Whether you're building your first program or your hundredth project, I hope you get to experience the same excitement that made me want to keep programming.
